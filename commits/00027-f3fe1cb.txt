Combat Rebuild Phase 1, step 1e-iii: migrate element abilities onto the pipeline
useElementAbility (combat.js) is kept as useElementAbilityLegacy (byte-
identical, the Layer 1 parity oracle); the live call site (parser.js's
element verbs) now resolves to a pipeline-backed definition. Stoneskin
(earth) is NOT migrated: a pure self-buff with no target roll at all,
same shape as Feint (step 1e-ii) -- handled inline in the orchestrator,
re-verified for regression safety.

The other seven elements (fire/water/lightning/acid/force/
transportation/air) share rollToHitPlayerActor/calculateDamagePlayerActor/
applyAndReactPlayerActor's existing dispatch (extended with a new
action.id branch each), reusing one factored-out copy of rollPlayerDamage's
core formula (rollPlayerDamageCoreForPipeline) with 1e-i's own two
stat()-purity peek/defer splits, plus each element's own damage
multiplier and side effect (fire's burn, water's heal + First Sword's
Regalia 10pc, acid's corrode + Defense penalty, force's stun, air's
evasion window, transportation's baked-in 1.3x). None of the seven ever
call applyPhysicalOnHitEffects, riposteCounterOnPlayer, or
arenaRiposteCounterOnPlayer -- a real, pre-existing gap the original
switch never had, preserved exactly.

One real shape difference from every other migrated player action:
useElementAbilityLegacy's own kill check happens AFTER its synergy
bonus and Spell Echo, both OUTSIDE its switch -- so
applyAndReactPlayerActor's element branch does not resolve the kill
itself; the orchestrator does, once, after synergy and Spell Echo have
both run, matching the original's exact sequencing. A miss also still
runs synergy/Spell Echo/the kill check (useElementAbilityLegacy's own
`break` out of the switch falls through to that same unconditional
tail, unlike every other action's early-return miss branch).

Lightning (Flurry) rolls its own formula twice, fully independently --
own crit roll, own Avatar of Creation stack bump, everything -- exactly
like the original's two back-to-back rollPlayerDamage calls. The SECOND
roll only happens in applyAndReactPlayerActor (step 5), gated on the
enemy still being alive after the first hit lands, not precomputed in
step 4: an earlier draft computed it unconditionally in step 4, which
silently inflated state.combat.hitsTakenFromPlayer by one unused count
whenever the first hit was already lethal. Layer 1's own
LIGHTNING-KILL-01 scenario didn't catch this -- a single-enemy fight,
where the kill ends combat and nulls state.combat before the extra
count could be observed -- but this step's Layer 2 check did: one boss-
sweep cell stopped matching exactly, bisected by seed to precisely this
case. Fixed, and the Layer 1 ledger's own setup switched to a two-enemy
fight so this class of bug can't hide behind a combat-ending kill
again.

Verification: 19 forced-roll scenarios (one hit/miss per element,
Lightning's double-roll and its kill-gated second hit, the shared
purity fixes, the synergy/Spell-Echo-before-kill-check ordering),
confirmed to actually discriminate via two deliberate sanity breaks
(reverted). Full suite: 348/348, audit 0 errors. Field ledger: 148/148
still open (pure control-flow restructure moves no fields). Layer 2:
boss sweep and the Dregnar/Arbitarius legal-party cells are an exact
bit-for-bit match against step 1d's own baseline, re-verified after the
Lightning fix above -- and genuinely exercised, since the tactical AI
actually casts every element in these sweeps.
