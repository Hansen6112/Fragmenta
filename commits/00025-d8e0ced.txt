formula, not a call to it, since it needs two things rollPlayerDamage

- The enemy's banked Brace/Guarded Strike/Sure Foundation Defense bonus

  a pure twin sharing effectivePlayerAtk's own formula (factored out

One real discrepancy surfaced by this step's own Layer 2 check (not by
Layer 1, which never exercises a mid-round retarget): playerAttackLegacy
captures its `creature` reference ONCE, before resolveSpeedInitiative
runs, and never refreshes it even though a faster enemy's pre-emptive
strike can itself kill the active enemy and move state.combat.activeIndex
on. Every stat read for the rest of that turn (Agility, Defense,
dmgReduction, banked bonuses, passives, element) keeps reading the
NOW-STALE creature, while the HP write and resolveKill go through
state.combat's own live proxy -- "whichever enemy is active right now."
A real, narrow, pre-existing engine quirk: a kill during the pre-emptive
phase can make the player's own attack compute damage off one creature's
defenses while the hit lands on a different one. My first draft built
the pipeline's target combatant fresh (post-pre-emptive-phase), which
read correctly instead -- an unintended fix, not a restructure, caught
when the boss sweep stopped matching exactly (one cell, one point of
average ally damage taken, isolated to a single seed via per-seed
bisection). Reproduced the quirk deliberately: the orchestrator now
builds a thin shim ("enemySlot") whose creatureObj is fixed to the
stale `creature` while its mutable fields (hp/maxHp/lastDamageType)
forward live to state.combat's own proxy -- bug-for-bug, not fixed here.
