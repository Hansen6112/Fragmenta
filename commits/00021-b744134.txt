Combat Rebuild Phase 1: path-difference register (handoff §3)
tools/combat_asymmetry_register.md -- one committed list of every known
difference between the enemy-attacks-player and enemy-attacks-ally
paths in pipeline.js (step 1c's own header comment, now cross-
referenced from there instead of only living in prose). Each row: the
mechanic, where it lives (file:line), its parity test if one exists, and
its kind per Tyler's three-way split (9 Nov):

  A: enemy-side rule, only fires vs the player today -> gets a U3
     switch, turned on per D2 (Marked Target, Flowing Discipline,
     Winter's Verdict, Toxic Preparation)
  B: player-only gear defense -> approved for Phase 2, not 1d
     (Reinforced, Timeless Guard, Guardian Spirit, Guided Footsteps,
     Never Where Expected, Blessing of Freedom, Avatar of Freedom,
     evasion charges/turns, Mercy of the Veil)
  C: deliberately ally-only, kept per D4 (Hadrian's protection, the
     generalized Ally Protection Reaction, companion damage mitigation,
     Last Bastion retaliation, Riposte-on-miss)

Plus the ally path's structural gap (no elemental/magic damage branch
at all) that U1/U2 target specifically, with Unquenched Focus called
out as the concrete thing U2 unblocks.

Two corrections made while actually re-reading the code line by line to
build this, rather than just transcribing the existing prose list:

- Stonewarden 6pc's first-hit-taken reduction was listed in pipeline.js's
  header as a player-only ASYMMETRY, but it's really "no defensive gear
  reduces an enemy's first hit on an ally at all" -- same shape as the
  kind-B table, not a kind-A enemy-side rule. Flagged needs-Tyler on
  which table it belongs in rather than guessing.
- Riposte-on-a-miss isn't actually ally-only: the player has the same
  gear effect (maybeRiposte, combat.js), just wired at a different layer
  (an outer call gated on retaliation.damage === 0, which covers every
  zero-damage case, not just a miss). The two wirings happen to cover
  the same ground today only because the ally path has none of the
  kind-B mechanics that would otherwise also reach zero damage --
  documented as a needs-Tyler follow-up for whenever Phase 2 extends any
  of those to allies.

Several rows (Marked Target, Flowing Discipline, Winter's Verdict, Toxic
Preparation, Mercy of the Veil) are asymmetric only by omission -- no
existing test asserts they *stay* absent on the ally path the way
P1C-NOREINFORCED-01/P1C-NOEVASION-01 do for the rest of the kind-B list.
Called out explicitly rather than implied covered.
