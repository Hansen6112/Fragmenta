is what "the harness never touches this content" looks like, not "R1
has no effect."

see this, use forced test cases instead"), applied here to R1:

- sim_common.js: a new setForcedPresence(holder, effectIds, setTierKeys)

  `afterParty` callback, run right after each fight's own freshly-

  party from scratch every fight).
