Phase 0 combat hotfixes: Dregnar targeting, special-creature metadata, boss-recruits-boss, level cap
Four fixes ahead of the combat rebuild, each verified against the live
code before and after:

1. Dregnar Ashvein's damage path (dregnarResolveDamagingAttempt) called
   resolveEnemyRetaliation unconditionally, bypassing pickEnemyAttackTarget
   entirely. He could never damage an ally, and kept hitting an already-
   downed player (confirmed via simulation: 0 ally-damage-taking fights,
   nonzero hits landed after the player went down). Now rolls a real
   target and routes to resolveEnemyAttackOnAlly when it lands on an ally.
   Ported his 5 on-hit riders (Ice-Forged Edge, Unbroken Grip, Executioner's
   Seal, Black-Ice Reserve's peek + Chilled-triggered grant) to that path;
   Unquenched Focus lives inside resolveEnemyRetaliation's elemental branch,
   which resolveEnemyAttackOnAlly has no equivalent of and which Dregnar's
   own (all-physical) kit never reaches on either path — left unported,
   flagging rather than silently skipping, per instructions.

2. special:true creatures had no level/dangerClass fields, so flee chance,
   XP, loot, notable-kill effects and Hadrian's Worthy Foe all silently
   treated Dregnar as a Normal at the player's own level (flee chance
   measured 60.5%, now 15% at level 25, matching the expected
   0.6-5*0.03-0.3). legacyScaledCreature now sets level from a new
   encounterLevel field without touching the flat x2 stat scale or routing
   dangerClass into stats. creaturesForTags gets a defensive !c.special
   guard against any future special creature leaking through a shared tag.

3. rollEncounterGroup let a Boss-tier anchor recruit other Bosses as
   packmates; at the top of a level band that could mean nothing but
   Bosses were left eligible. Boss-or-higher anchors now require strictly
   lower-rank packmates; Normal/Elite anchors are unchanged. Confirmed
   Arbitarius Commander now spawns solo across 200 rolls (was regularly
   3-5 simultaneous bosses).

4. Capped six bosses' levelRange max at 30 (enemy cap), keeping each
   minimum: roadlord, vigilis_unit_commander, militia_warleader,
   watch_captain, ovum_champion, arbitarius_commander. Added an audit.js
   assertion so levelRange/encounterLevel over 30 fails the build.

Retest (player level 25): Dregnar win rate 0%/0%/92%/100% for
solo/+1 ally/+Kessa+Hadrian/+Kessa+Hadrian+3 mercs, with every multi-ally
fight now showing real ally damage taken and zero hits landing on an
already-downed player. Arbitarius Commander solo win rate 2.5%. Full
regression suite and audit.js unchanged from baseline.
