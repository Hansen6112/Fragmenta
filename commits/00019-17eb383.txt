Combat Rebuild Phase 1, step 1c: extend the pipeline to the enemy-to-ally path
Extends the five shared pipeline functions (js/engine/pipeline.js, step
1b) with the enemy-attacking-an-ally branch, migrated verbatim from
resolveEnemyAttackOnAlly (combat.js) -- same arithmetic, same effect
order, nothing rebalanced. The old function is renamed
resolveEnemyAttackOnAllyLegacy and kept byte-for-byte unchanged as the
parity oracle; the new pipeline-backed resolveEnemyAttackOnAlly (same
name, same signature, so all 4 real call sites needed no edits at all)
is defined in pipeline.js and wins by load order.

Two centralized functions extracted along the way, since 1c is the step
that reaches their call site (an ally defending against an enemy attack):
effectiveAllyAgility (companion.js, new -- pure extract from the inline
calc, zero behavior change) and effectiveAllyDef (already existed,
companion.js), both now wired into stat()'s ally branch
(js/engine/combatant.js). Ally atk/acc (the ally ATTACKING, not
defending) stay unmigrated until step 1d, same disclosure as step 1a.

The player-attacking and ally-attacking paths are NOT symmetric in the
original game, and this step preserves every one of those asymmetries
exactly rather than quietly "completing" them (a balance question for
later, not Phase 1's call): Reinforced/the stun gate/Timeless Guard/
Guardian Spirit/Guided Footsteps/Never Where Expected/Blessing or Avatar
of Freedom/evasion charges or windows/Marked Target/Flowing Discipline/
Winter's Verdict/Stonewarden's physical reduction/Toxic Preparation only
ever applied targeting the player; Hadrian's protection, the generalized
Ally Protection Reaction, companion damage mitigation, Last Bastion
retaliation, and Riposte-on-miss only ever applied targeting an ally.
Each asymmetry has its own preserved-on-purpose test. Also moved the
ability heal-on-hit write off the old combat.hp activeIndex proxy onto
actor.store.hp/maxHp directly, same fix step 1b already made for Mirror
Soul.
