Two bugs found and fixed in the mechanical rewrite script along the way:

1. The regex `\b(state\.combat|combat)\.FIELD\b` anchored on only the two
   literal prefixes "state.combat" and "combat", so a longer chain like
   `h.state.combat.FIELD` (data/actions.js's PIPELINE_HOOKS, which holds
   its state as `h.state`) matched starting mid-chain at "state" and
   silently dropped the "h." prefix, producing broken calls like
   `h.activeEnemySlot(state.combat).burn` (passes `node -c`, throws at
   runtime). Caught by reviewing the diff before committing; the first
   attempt at this batch was reverted in full and the script's regex was
   changed to capture the whole dotted chain ending in ".combat"
   (`((?:[A-Za-z_$][\w$]*\.)*combat)`) and reuse it verbatim.

2. The negative lookahead added to avoid rewriting object-literal keys
   (`hp: 5`) also incorrectly suppressed a ternary's "? combat.hp : 0" --
   same "FIELD immediately followed by optional whitespace then a colon"
   shape. This left one stale read in tools/tests/downed_ledger.js
   (`const enemyHpAfter = s.combat ? s.combat.hp : 0;`), caught by a
   dedicated repo-wide grep for the pattern after the main rewrite and
   fixed by hand (confirmed to be the only occurrence anywhere in the
   repo). The script's guard was tightened to require an actual
   terminator (`,` or `}`) after the colon's value before treating it as
   an object-literal key.

All ~130 production-file occurrences were already confirmed (prior
commits 5cadf8f, 7917001) to need no activeIndex-mutation caching

statement, with no span of code between two accesses that could itself
move activeIndex.
