1f-iii §4 (part 1): ally-protection interceptions as named hooks
Per the step 1f handoff's §4: "Interceptions (applyAllyProtectionReaction,
maybeHadrianAllyProtection, enemyAllyProtectionMultiplier) become
onIncomingDamage hooks on the defender or protector, running inside
step 5 before HP changes."

- js/data/actions.js: two new PIPELINE_HOOKS entries
  (hadrianAllyProtection/allyProtectionReaction), each a thin by-name
  wrapper around the real, unchanged companion.js/combat.js function —
  same convention as 1f-i's hadrianFlatAtkBonus wrapper. A new declared,
  ordered list, ON_INCOMING_DAMAGE_ALLY_HOOKS, names them in the exact
  order step 5 already ran them (Legion's Memory/Brothers in Arms before
  the plain Ally Protection Reaction — order matters, since Legion's
  Memory redirects part of the hit before the generic 0.9x reduction
  would otherwise apply to the full amount).
- js/engine/pipeline.js: applyAndReact's ally-target branch (step 5) now
  loops over that declared list by name instead of calling
  maybeHadrianAllyProtection/applyAllyProtectionReaction directly,
  hardcoded. Companion Ability Engine mitigation (applyCompanionDamage
  Taken) stays a direct call -- it's an always-on mechanic, not a
  reaction with its own trigger/cap.

Disclosed interpretation: maybeHadrianAllyProtection's own "is Hadrian
in the party" check is UNCHANGED and still lives inside that function —
per the handoff's own "kind-C mechanics ... keep exactly their current
behaviour. Only their shape changes," this is Hadrian's own legitimately
unique mechanic, not the step-function ally-identity dispatch P4
forbids (that rule is about pipeline.js/PIPELINE_HOOKS never branching
on identity, not every helper anywhere in the engine never mentioning

already a plain multiplicative factor folded directly into
PIPELINE_FORMULAS.playerCore's own sangMit chain (rollPlayerDamageCore
ForPipeline), reading hasAllyProtectionEffect/cooldown/uses fields
generically with zero identity branching — already compliant, nothing
to move.
