Damage Types follow-ups: mainhand rule, reapply-based escalation, D1 fix
Three follow-ups to 65fa230, all behind the switch except the first
(data):

1. Loaded the 6 mainhand items that were incorrectly left untyped
   (caravan guard saber/red banner greatsword -> slashing; river focus
   staff/crystal focus rod/Lantern of Quiet Passage/Codex of Infinite
   Horizons -> blunt), per the new rule: every mainhand item has a
   damage type, only an offhand item can be "(not a weapon)". Corrects
   the 6 CSV rows (they'd carried the OFFHAND reasoning template on a
   mainhand row -- the actual generator bug) and tightens
   load_damage_types_content.js to throw on a repeat of that mistake
   instead of silently skipping it.

2. Status escalation (Chilled -> Frozen, Drowsy -> Asleep) now fires on
   REAPPLICATION while the status is already active (T8's own "build-
   up" wording), not at natural expiry -- the previous pass had this
   backwards. Reworked: removed the on-expiry dispatch from
   tickPlayerStatuses/tickAllyStatuses entirely; applyPlayerStatus/
   applyAllyStatus now check (via the new escalatedStatusOnReapply,
   shared by both) whether the status being applied is already active,
   and escalate instead of just refreshing its duration. Restricted to
   non-stackable statuses so Constrict/Blight Seed's own untouched
   stack-based escalation can't be reached by this path. Tests updated:
   a single Chilled still expires harmlessly; a second frost hit while
   Chilled gives Frozen; a second Drowsy gives Asleep.

3. The 12 "BESTIARY.* has no attackType and no equipped mainhand
   weapon" D1 warnings from 65fa230 were a false positive in my own
   audit rule, not a real gap: all 12 (caravan_sellsword,
   veteran_merchant_guard, veteran_fedahl, bandit_cutthroat,
   highway_cutthroat, ferratum_veteran, aqualis_marine, gradus_veteran,
   ductus_section_commander, veteran_city_guard, rural_militia_fighter,
   road_convoy_guard) carry a real typed mainhand at every level in
   their range via a level-banded `equipmentBands` array
   (resolveEnemyEquipment, js/engine/combat.js, already reads this) --
   the D1 rule only ever checked the flat `equipment.mainhand` shape,
   never equipmentBands. Fixed to check both, and to separately warn if
   any individual band is ever missing its own mainhand.

Full suite 519/519 (20/20 in the content-load ledger, now including
the mainhand-rule and reapply-escalation cases), audit 0 errors / 229
warnings (18 fewer than 65fa230 -- exactly the 6 weapon + 12 creature
false positives this fixes). Switch off throughout; off-switch
behavior is unchanged.
