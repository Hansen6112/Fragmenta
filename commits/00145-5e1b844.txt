2f: enemy passives -- flatStatBonus (10)
10 ids, each a constant stat bonus applied once at creature-build time
(buildFightCreature, combat.js). The field to add to (p.stat) stays
read directly off the data; only the flat amount moves into the
registry, dispatched by key.

Deliberately reused "fight.start" rather than any stat.<key> hook:
stat.<key>'s own fan-out (applyStatDefFloor, modifiers.js) genuinely
reaches holderRole "enemy" via combatant.js's stat() "def" case, and
collects results before filtering by statBonus/statMultiplier -- a
plain-number entry there would have silently overridden the enemy's
entire effective Defense on every read instead of adding to it.
fight.start's one fan-out call site hardcodes holderRole "player",
same precedent as combatStartDebuff/combatStartSelfBuff (2f batch 3).
