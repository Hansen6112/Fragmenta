Enforce party cap: at most 3 allies (player + 3, a party of four)
Party Cap request. recruitAlly had no limit; the player plus 3
companions (Tyler's design) was never actually enforced anywhere.

- state.js: new MAX_PARTY_ALLIES = 3. recruitAlly returns null once
  the party already has 3 LIVING members — counted against alive
  members specifically, not raw array length: a fallen ally stays in
  state.party (alive: false) until the player resolves them with 'send
  <name> home' or 'leave <name>' (both confirmed to splice them out —
  cmdSendAllyHome/cmdLeaveAllyBody), so counting every entry regardless
  of alive would block a new recruit during that brief unresolved
  window even though a slot is, functionally, already free.

  already full." and leaves kessaQuestReady alone, so talking to her

  either of Hadrian's two offers is a one-time event once its flag
  clears, so a full-party refusal has to leave the flag untouched too,
  or the player would permanently lose him just for having a full party
  at the wrong moment.
- parser.js (cmdRevive): extended the existing pre-flight check (already
  there to avoid spending a revival item/favor on a doomed recruitAlly
  call) with the same full-party condition, so a full party costs
  nothing here either.
