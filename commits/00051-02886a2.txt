Phase 1, step 1f-iii: maybeRiposte (player's own Riposte vs an enemy)
Found while scoping audit rule P1 (the next 1f task): the handoff's §4
reaction list names "riposteCounterOnPlayer, riposteCounterByAllyOnEnemy,
riposteCounterByEnemyOnAlly, arenaRiposteCounterOnPlayer, maybeRiposte,
companion retaliation, Last Bastion" -- six of those seven got converted
during 1f-iii's own work, but maybeRiposte (the PLAYER's OWN Riposte
gear, countering an ENEMY -- the mirror-image direction of
riposteCounterOnPlayer, which is an ENEMY wearer countering the player)
was missed. P1 would have had to carry a second "temporary" exemption
for it alongside Dregnar's, which the handoff only asked for one of --
the right fix is finishing 1f-iii's own scope, not widening P1's
allow-list for a gap 1f-iii left behind.

Eligibility ("does the player even have Riposte, is the target already
dead") stays in combat.js's own maybeRiposte, outside the pipeline, same
as every other reaction's wrapper. The roll/damage/kill now runs through
a new resolveRiposteCounterPlayer, steps 2-5, with two different reuse
decisions depending on how much baggage each shared step function
actually carries:

- rollToHitPlayerActor (step 3) is reused AS-IS, through the ordinary
  top-level rollToHit dispatch -- its entire body is the one
  accuracy-vs-agility check maybeRiposte always made itself, with
  nothing else in it to leak.
- calculateDamagePlayerActor/applyAndReactPlayerActor (steps 4-5) are
  NOT reused: the former unconditionally runs
  rollPlayerDamageCoreForPipeline (which defers Brace/Guarded Strike/
  Sure Foundation consumption to the latter) and the latter
  unconditionally consumes a Siege Corps atk charge whenever the hit is
  physical -- gated on dmgInfo.isPhysical, not on any action field a
  reaction could opt out of, so there's no safe way to reuse it without
  a leak. Both get a dedicated formula (PIPELINE_FORMULAS.riposteCounterPlayer,
  js/data/actions.js) and tail (applyAndReactReactionOnEnemy, pipeline.js)
  instead, new top-of-function guards in calculateDamage/applyAndReact
  routing actor.kind === "player" && target.kind === "enemy" reactions
  to them. The new formula deliberately keeps calling rollPlayerDamage
  (the ORIGINAL function maybeRiposte has always called, still
  separately maintained from rollPlayerDamageCoreForPipeline since
  1e-i), not the newer core -- avoiding any question of whether the two
  have ever silently drifted. White Watch 6pc's stacking Defense bonus
  moved into the formula too (unobservable: it never depended on
  anything the write step computes, and a miss already never reached it
  either way).

maybeRiposte itself was never duplicated under a Legacy name -- every
caller, old (playerAttackLegacy/useDecoyLegacy/useDisarmLegacy/
useElementAbilityLegacy) and live (useItem/useFeint/useApothecaryAbility/
pipeline.js's own orchestrators), has always called the same shared
function, so no call site needed to change.

New dedicated test file (phase1_step1f_iii_player_riposte_pipeline_parity.js,
8 scenarios, registered in run_all.js): no Legacy oracle exists for this
one either, so each scenario pins the new path against a hand-computed
expected value (same convention as the ticks suite) -- the exact math
match against rollPlayerDamage * riposteMultiplier * clashMult on an
identical forced RNG sequence, White Watch's multiplier AND its Defense
stack, the kill path, and the no-activeElement call shape (a mage's
riposte is always "physical").

Verified: audit.js (0 errors, P4 at 0), full suite (402/402, up from
394 -- the 8 new scenarios), and both whole-fight harnesses bit-for-bit
identical against the prior 1f-iv baseline.
