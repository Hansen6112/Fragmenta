Consumables: don't consume the item when its effect would do nothing
applyConsumableEffect now returns { lines, consumed } instead of a bare
line array; both useItem call sites (the out-of-combat and the
mid-combat paths) only splice the item out of state.inventory when
consumed is true, instead of unconditionally beforehand.

- heal: already at full Health (and no Overflowing Life-style
  conversion into Temporary Health, which still counts as something
  real happening) -> "You're already at full health — X would do
  nothing right now." Not consumed.
- cleanse: nothing active to clear -> "There's nothing to clear right
  now — X would do nothing." Not consumed. Also fixes a real crash:
  using a cleanse potion OUTSIDE combat previously read
  state.combat.playerStatuses on a null state.combat.
- injuryDowngrade (the field splint kit): no severe, untreated injury
  -> "There's nothing a splint can help with right now." Not consumed.
  Collapses the two previous distinct messages (no severe injury at
  all / every severe one already field-treated) into this one, per
  request.
- buff is unaffected — it always has somewhere to apply, no no-op case.

In-combat turn economy is unchanged: using an item mid-fight still
spends the turn (beginTurn/resolveSpeedInitiative already ran before
the item's own effect resolves) even when its effect turns out to do
nothing — only whether the physical item is consumed changes.

Verified with a 9-case test (heal at full HP in and out of combat kept
vs. spent correctly, cleanse with/without an active status, cleanse
outside combat no longer crashes, both field-splint-kit no-target
sub-cases now refuse without consuming) plus the existing Injury-System
(24/24), downed-system (17/17), and ally-protection (9/9) ledgers,
tools/audit.js, and combatsim.js, all still clean. Updated one existing
test (injury_items_retest's SPLINT-02) that had asserted the prior
always-consumed behavior as deliberate and correct — it was, at the
time; this commit is what changes it.
