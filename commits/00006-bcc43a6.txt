Implement Injury System (spec v0.2/v0.3): persistent injuries from survived downs, rally mechanic, 7th-fall death
- New per-down injury system: surviving a knockdown (win, flee, or a
  Mugamiir dungeon rescue) banks the hit's damage type as a down, applied
  at fight-end as one of 13 slot injuries across 6 damage-type groups
  (minor -10%/severe -25% to one targeted stat, multiplicative with
  fatigue; two slots target max Health, ground fatigue never touches).
  Capped at 3 held injuries / injury load 6; a 7th fall is fatal outright,
  even inside a dungeon.
- Rally mechanic: the first ally to act each round, while the player is
  downed, spends their turn using a "smelling salts"-type item from the
  player's own inventory to bring them back up at 25% max Health, instead
  of their normal action.
- Apothecary field-treatment consumable downgrades one severe injury to
  minor (never removes it), once per injury, outside combat only.
- Town healer (cmdHeal) now offers a cure menu (one injury at a time, or
  all at once) whenever the player is carrying any, alongside the
  existing plain HP heal.
- applyHeal is now a no-op while the player is downed, so passive/
  set-bonus auto-heals can't quietly revive or top off a downed player
  ahead of a real rally.
- Moved the Mugamiir Safor dungeon rescue out of checkDeathPrevention's
  unconditional fallback and into the actual party-wipe branches of
  resolvePlayerDefeatOrDowned/killAlly, so a dungeon party can go down
  and get carried like anywhere else instead of never going down at all.
- D14: the hit that causes a party wipe is itself banked as a down when
  a dungeon rescue follows, not just the downs that happened earlier in
  the fight.
- recordPlayerDeath now records why (ordinary combat vs. the 7th-fall
  injury death) via state.flags.pendingDeathCause.

Verified against the existing downed-system, ally-protection, and
Dregnar Stage A/B regression ledgers (clean, two pre-existing unrelated
failures confirmed via stash-compare against the prior commit) plus a
new 24-case Injury System acceptance ledger covering assignment rules,
the 7th-fall check inside and outside dungeons, the rally mechanic,
healer/field-treatment cures, and save/load round-tripping.
