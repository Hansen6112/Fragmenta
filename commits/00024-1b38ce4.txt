Combat Rebuild Phase 1, step 1e handoff §0: sort the Hadrian-vs-Kessa carry-over
Step 1d's report flagged five things Hadrian's kit-bearing attack is
missing that the plain ally's (Kessa) attack has. Sorted all five into
the asymmetry register per the step 1e handoff:

- Banked Defense (Brace/Guarded Strike/Sure Foundation), Smuggler's
  Guard/Commander's Poise mitigation, and Riposte-on-miss are all the
  ENEMY's own kit -- same D2 principle as the rest of U3 ("an enemy's
  kit applies to whatever it hits" extends to "and whatever hits it").
  Each now has a U3 switch (U3e/U3f/U3g, off by default, same
  convention as U3a-d): calculateDamageAllyActor's/rollToHitAllyActor's/
  applyAndReactAllyActor's kit branches in pipeline.js.

- Opening Reach and the Executioner multiplier are the ATTACKING ally's
  OWN gear bonus, not an enemy rule -- they don't fit U3's shape. Added
  a new register kind, D ("ally kit difference"), deferred to Phase 2
  alongside kind B. Moot in practice today: Hadrian's gear is lore-fixed
  named equipment (js/data/hadrian.js), not generic hasEffect-tagged
  gear, so this only matters if his deferred Legendary ascension ever
  adds one of these effect ids.
