Source: `Continental_Geography_Travel_Reference.docx` (extracted text). ## 1. Continent Overview

- **Single major continent**, surrounded by ocean, holding **five nations**. The island nation of **Norrvael** lies off the eastern coast, reached only by two controlled bridge systems (not organically "off-map" — it's politically and physically gated).
- **Canonical dimensions:**
  - North–South (continent): ~2,800 miles — from Sanguivorum's tundra coast to Thraekor's southern fault cliffs.
  - East–West (continent): ~2,000 miles — from western coastlines to Sahrimor's eastern desert edge.
  - Norrvael (island), N–S: ~450 miles.
  - Norrvael (island), E–W: ~180 miles at widest.
  - Continent-to-Norrvael strait: ~20–40 miles, crossed via the **Kolven** and **Skarveld** bridge systems.
- **Comparative scale:** roughly the size of the Indian subcontinent. A full circuit of all major nations on the fastest reliable roads takes **~8 months**.
- **Strategic implication:** nations are not "casual neighbours." Sanguivorum's north to Khar-Athel (deep south, Thraekor) is a coast-to-coast-scale journey. Wars are constrained by supply lines; couriers on Sanguivorum's network cover 40–50 mi/day, so news from the far north takes 3–4 weeks to reach the southern border.

## 2. The Five Nations (Major Landmasses / Political-Geographic Blocs)

### SANGUIVORUM — "The Empire of the Meadows"
- **Terrain:** Open plains/farmland (heartland), tundra (far north), river corridors (east), fjord coastline (west).
- **Character:** The most road-friendly nation on the continent by far. Open meadow imposes no travel penalty; the empire's **Ingenum-engineered roads** add a further +20% speed on top of open terrain, and drain/hold up better than any other surface on the continent (mud season resistant).
- **Sub-regions:**
  - **Heartland** — open plains, best travel conditions anywhere in the world, cavalry terrain.
  - **Far north tundra** — firm but modestly slowing in summer (70% speed); in **deep winter** collapses to ×0.45 for wagons (sledges partially compensate; seasonal depot system exists).
  - **Western fjord coastline** — NOT navigable overland; channels too deep/narrow to ford, too sheer to descend. Controlled absolutely by the **Legionus Aqualis** (a naval/coastal faction/military body). Coastal travel here means sea travel under their control.
  - **Southern border with Vaeloris** — sharp terrain transition from forest to plains; merchants coming from Vaeloris's forest roads find the open plains "disorienting in their ease."
- **Faction of note:** Legionus Aqualis (controls fjord waters).
- **Named locations:** Arethon, Apollyon, Zaeveon, Tritoneth (see gazetteer below).

### VAELORIS — "The Ancient Forest & The Swamp"
- **Terrain:** Ancient dense forest (north/centre), wetland and deep swamp (south).
- **Character:** The hardest overland travel environment on the continent for outsiders. Elves maintain forest paths deliberately non-intuitive to non-initiates. The **Sylvara river** is the primary and fastest travel corridor through the nation.
- **Sub-regions:**
  - **Ancient dense forest** (north of The Arbor) — 55% of open-road speed for those who know the paths; worse/riskier for those who don't.
  - **Wetland/swamp edge** (southern reaches) — 50% speed, wagons effectively impossible beyond the edge.
  - **Deep swamp** (Drath Vorrumbruk region) — 30% speed, navigable only with a local guide; wagons impossible, horses dangerous. Inhabited/guided by **lizardfolk**, who do not advertise their navigational knowledge to outsiders.
- **River:** Sylvara — downstream 55–70 mi/day, upstream 25–35 mi/day (river vessel). Route: The Rivers Gate (north) → Sylmere → past The Arbor → south toward Drathmere and The Confluence. Spring flood makes upstream travel impassable but boosts downstream speed ×1.2 (some sections may become impassable regardless).
- **Faction/culture of note:** Elves (forest path-keepers), lizardfolk (swamp guides).
- **Named locations:** The Rivers Gate, The Arbor, The Red Hold, Sylmere, Drathmere, The Confluence, Drath Vorrumbruk.

### SAHRIMOR — "The Desert Commercial Empire"
- **Terrain:** Desert (majority), canyon terrain (eastern interior, Kharzun region), isolated mountain mass (Nocthera, far north), coastal desert meeting ocean (south/west).
- **Character:** Governed above all by water and knowledge. Not uniformly hostile — navigable for those who know it, lethal for those who don't. Nine cities total (only some named in the route tables). Commercial infrastructure run by the **Merchant Lords**.
- **Sub-regions:**
  - **Established caravan routes** — well-worn, water-pointed, maintained by Merchant Lord infrastructure; ~55–65% of road speed with proper water prep. One day off-route without planned water sources = survival danger within 48 hours.
  - **Night travel** is standard in hot months — travel compresses to ~8 effective hours/day (rest mid-morning to late afternoon, move at dawn/evening).
  - **Kharzun canyon terrain** (eastern interior) — requires pack animals/camels, not wagons; Kharzun itself is built vertically into canyon walls with switchback approach routes impassable to wheeled vehicles.
  - **The Black Sands** (deep desert south of established routes) — functionally impassable to all experienced travellers; unnaturally cold against surrounding heat; inhabited by creatures of increasing size the deeper one goes; has consumed every expedition attempting systematic exploration. No commercial route or Merchant Lord caravan goes there. Treated as the end of the known world's southern reach.
- **Faction of note:** Merchant Lords; House Massa (commercial infrastructure specifically at Nyssara).
- **Named locations:** Sahurim, Nyssara, Nocthera, Kharzun (and 5 more of Sahrimor's "nine cities" unnamed in this document).

### THRAEKOR — "The Ash Confederation"
- **Terrain:** Volcanic mountain range (interior), lava plains (southwest, around Khar-Athel), fault cliff coast (south — absolutely impassable), contested coastline (west), open transitional ground (north/east, toward Vaeloris and the strait).
- **Character:** Not built for outsiders. The volcanic interior is its most effective natural defense — not hostile by design, just deeply unfamiliar to non-natives. **Dwarves** know the stable ground, which lava-field paths are reliable vs. shifting, and where geothermal vents make tunnels impassable.
- **Sub-regions:**
  - **Volcanic interior** — 20–35% of normal speed without a dwarf guide; genuine casualty risk from unstable terrain.
  - **Northern/eastern approaches** (toward Vaeloris forest edge and the strait coast) — much easier, near-normal speed on maintained paths between holds.
  - **Southern fault coast** — absolute wall. Sheer cliffs straight into ocean; no beach, no anchorage, no landing possible anywhere. No army has ever landed here; ships that get close enough to inspect it don't report a way through.
  - **Western coastline** (around Ashvel and Keth-Dur) — navigable; Thraekor's primary sea-trade contact point. Described as **Low Hold territories** — contested internally, but accessible.
  - **Eruption events** render active lava fields impassable with no advance warning system for outsiders. Post-eruption ashfall reduces speed ×0.8–0.9 and affects health/forage if prolonged.
- **Entry points for outsiders:** Dunnrak in the north (overland from Vaeloris); Ashvel or Keth-Dur by sea (western coast). All other approaches controlled by specific holds with variable welcome, or geographically impossible.
- **Factions/clans of note:** Dwarves (mountain-dwelling natives); Low Hold territories (contested internal holds); **Keth-Orn clan** (controls the official Dunnrak border crossing — terms of crossing have changed three times in the last decade).
- **Named locations:** Khar-Athel, Thurak, Dunnrak, Ashvel, Keth-Dur, Vorreth.

### NORRVAEL — "The Island Nation"
- **Terrain:** Mountain range (central spine), cliff harbours (coastal), canyon roads (interior east–west crossings), managed forest (Askveld region), high plateau (Isthvar), mist-shrouded surrounding ocean.
- **Character:** Island-first geography. Access controlled by exactly two bridge systems:
  - **Kolven bridge** (north) — connects to Sahrimor via **House Massa's** commercial infrastructure at **Nyssara**.
  - **Skarveld bridge** (west) — connects to the Thraekor side of the strait.
  - Both crossings are controlled, logged, and subject to halt without notice or justification. **There is no third entry point.**
- **The Mist:** surrounds the island's sea approaches; not ordinary weather — routes that work on one crossing may not work on the next attempt. Norrvael's licensed pilots know the channels but their services are neither publicly offered nor cheap. Hiring one adds 1–3 days to any approach but removes the navigational hazard.
- **Internal geography:** dominated by the central mountain spine; cities sit at canyon mouths, cliff harbours, mountain shelves. The **canyon road from Kolven to Drakenholm** is the island's commercial spine (most-used internal route). Branching routes to **Runholm, Askveld, and Stormven** are well-maintained. **Isthvar** (high plateau) and **Vorrkar** (southwest) require deliberate navigation — built to be difficult to reach.
- **Named locations:** Kolven, Drakenholm, Runholm, Askveld, Stormven, Isthvar, Vorrkar, Skarveld (bridge/crossing point).

All times are **one-way**. Round trips double the figure plus rest time; experienced merchants plan a minimum of 10 days rest/resupply in a foreign city before returning. Pad schedules 30–40% for domestic travel, 50–60% for foreign travel (unplanned delays, encounters, politics, bad guides).

## 5. Borders, Territories & Factions

- **Sanguivorum ↔ Vaeloris** border at **The Rivers Gate**: the most complex crossing on the continent. The **Kabal bridge** exists here as a **sovereign third party** (i.e., not controlled by either nation) — commercial "grey market" activity means logged crossings and actual crossings aren't always the same thing.
- **Norrvael's two bridges** (Kolven, Skarveld): the most rigidly controlled crossings on the continent. Nothing crosses without the garrison seeing/recording it; the garrison commander's judgment on what's permitted is final and unreviewable.
- **Thraekor's Dunnrak crossing**: controlled by the **Keth-Orn clan**; terms have changed three times in the last decade — unstable/negotiable border regime.
- **Sahrimor's desert crossings**: formally open to any paying traveller (the Merchant Lords don't turn away business), but practically gated by the need for a water guide and route knowledge — accessible mainly to those with existing relationships or capital to hire the right people at **Nyssara** or **Nocthera**.
- **House Massa** — controls commercial infrastructure at Nyssara (the Sahrimor-side bridgehead to Norrvael).
- **Legionus Aqualis** — controls Sanguivorum's fjord coastline waters absolutely.
- **Merchant Lords** — commercial governing body of Sahrimor; run the caravan/waterpoint infrastructure across the nation's nine cities.
- **Low Hold territories** — Thraekor's western coastal holds (around Ashvel, Keth-Dur); internally contested but accessible to outsiders.
- **Dwarves** — the native culture of Thraekor's volcanic interior (and implied by extension the "known guides" for Norrvael's mountainous mist-adjacent terrain, though not explicitly stated for Norrvael).
- **Elves** — maintain and gatekeep the ancient forest paths of Vaeloris.
- **Lizardfolk** — inhabit and guide travel through Vaeloris's deep swamp (Drath Vorrumbruk); do not share navigational knowledge with outsiders.
- **Ingenum** — credited as the engineers/builders of Sanguivorum's road network (possibly a guild, order, or historical figure/group — the doc doesn't elaborate further).

## 6. Dangers, Resources & Points of Interest (by region)

- **Sanguivorum:**
  - Danger: deep winter tundra travel without preparation is "genuine difficulty"; fjord coast is a sheer, ocean-swallowing hazard for the unprepared (and controlled by the Legionus Aqualis regardless).
  - Resource/advantage: best roads and travel infrastructure on the continent; full relay-courier network with fresh horses at waypoints (unique to Sanguivorum).
- **Vaeloris:**
  - Danger: getting lost in the ancient forest without knowing elf-maintained paths; the deep swamp (Drath Vorrumbruk) is guide-only, horse travel "dangerous," wagon travel impossible.
  - Resource: the Sylvara river as a fast, high-volume travel/trade corridor (55–70 mi/day downstream).
- **Sahrimor:**
  - Danger: **The Black Sands** — cold, monster-infested (creatures of "increasing size the further one penetrates"), has consumed every serious expedition; off-route desert travel risks death by dehydration within 48 hours.
  - Resource: nine-city commercial network, established waterpoint caravan routes, camel-based canyon trade at Kharzun.
- **Thraekor:**
  - Danger: volcanic interior — unstable ground, ashfall, geothermal vents, active eruptions that can close routes with no warning; the southern fault coast is a total travel wall (no landing possible, ever).
  - Resource: dwarven mountain-guide knowledge (only way to safely traverse volcanic interior/lava fields); western coastal sea trade via Ashvel/Keth-Dur.
- **Norrvael:**
  - Danger: the surrounding mist — unpredictable, non-repeating sea routes outside the two bridge corridors; Isthvar (plateau) and Vorrkar (southwest) are deliberately hard to reach.
  - Resource: well-maintained internal canyon-road network (commercial spine Kolven–Drakenholm); licensed pilots who can neutralize the mist hazard for a fee (1–3 extra days).
