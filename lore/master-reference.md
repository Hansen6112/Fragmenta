# FRAGMENTA — Master World Reference Digest

**One-sentence summary:** Fragmenta is a grimdark high-fantasy setting in which a dead thirteen-headed god's shattered horn seeded the world with magic and dangerous relics, while its secretly still-conscious remains are being deliberately gathered by the immortal head of the world's magical superpower (the Gods' Hand Kabal) toward his god's resurrection — a plan hidden beneath the surface politics of five very differently-run nations (Roman-esque Sanguivorum, elven/lizardfolk/dragonborn Vaeloris, dwarven Thraekor, drake-bonded island Norrvael, and merchant-empire Sahrimor).

## 1. CORE PREMISE / COSMOLOGY

**Why "Fragmenta":** Before existence there was only **the Idea** — paradoxical, alone, complete yet fractured. It tore itself into thirteen faces/consciousnesses, each holding a "horn of power," becoming the **Thirteen-Headed God / First Divinity / Absolute Prime**. **The First Head** dominated the other twelve by force, and under its tyranny the Thirteen forged the **Spheres of Creation** (Light/Shadow, Life/Death, War/Peace, Knowledge/Mystery, Dream/Form, Growth/Ruin, Time/Timelessness, Magic/Void) — reality itself. As the First Head began *consuming* rather than merely ruling creation, the other twelve poured all their power into its horn to overload it. **The Shattering**: the horn exploded not into matter but into *concepts* — ideas, memories, timelines flung across the cosmos. The First Head died ("erased yet screaming... a paradox undone"), leaving a titanic skull with thirteen horns (one shattered to the root): **the Dead God / the Silent One / the Lost Thirteenth**. The surviving Twelve fled into mortal hosts and were reborn as the **Pantheon of Twelve** — explicitly "not siblings, not allies, not friends — survivors of a god they had murdered." The buried skull sits beneath the Kabal's Tower, in a chamber called **the Sepulcher**; it is "not gone. Just waiting. Remembering in pieces. And someday — reassembling."

**The Fragmenta** (title concept): the scattered shard-pieces of the Thirteenth's consciousness/power, which "do not wake unless found" and adapt to whoever finds them. Tiered: **Fragmenta Majoris** (catastrophic, god-detectable), **Fragmenta Minoris** (corrupts institutions/guilds/tribes), **Fragmenta Motus** (dust-like, causes tiny luck/talent shifts, invisible to gods). Fragments never seek each other (each thinks itself the center) but **fuse instantly and irreversibly** if brought together, spiking power and divine visibility. **Voidmasking** is the forbidden concealment art (taught by the Thirteenth to Primus) that uses the "static" of scattered Fragmenta to blind the gods to what's really happening.

**Central conflict:** **Archmagi Primus**, immortal founder/leader of the **Gods' Hand Kabal**, carries a fragment of the Thirteenth in his body and has spent centuries (including engineering the **Great Mage War**'s end and annihilating his own homeland, the **Golden Plains**, to seize the skull) gathering and fusing Fragmenta Majoris to resurrect the Thirteenth. He believes he will ascend to godhood; the Thirteenth actually intends to consume him as a new body. Meanwhile the Twelve sense "wrongness" in the world but, blinded by Voidmasking, suspect only each other (a "divine cold war").

**Tone/genre:** Grimdark high fantasy layered with cosmic horror and constant dramatic irony — nearly every nation's proud official history conceals Kabal manipulation, genocide, or a buried truth known only to the reader/GM.

## 2. MAJOR REGIONS, NATIONS, FACTIONS, GOVERNMENTS

Single continent (~2,000 mi east-west) plus the island **Norrvael**; **the Golden Tear** (Kabal sovereign territory, home to the 1,000-ft obsidian **Kabal Tower**) sits at the center, bordered by Sanguivorum, Vaeloris, and Sahrimor.

- **The Gods' Hand Kabal** — supranational magical authority, ruled by **Archmagi Primus** and **the Five Fingers**: **Secundus** ("the Pointer," battle master), **Tertius** ("the Middle," diplomat, true name **Dûrinne Stonebrow**), **Quartus** ("the Ring," public healer/secret experimenter running the **Garden of Remade Souls**), **Quintus** ("the Pinky," dragonborn teleportation master, works from the **Apex Chamber/Observatory of Doors**). Enforces **Three Laws of Magic**; funds itself via mage registration, the **Mage Tithe** (10%), and bridge tolls; designates rogue mages **"Bruises"** (killable on sight).

- **Sanguivorum — "The Empire of the Meadows"** (570 years old, Roman/Greek-inspired, 12 cities/12 Legions). Ruled by **Imperator Civis Alastair**, tiered Senate (Supremus/Ascendus/Eminants), core maxim *"A citizen is a tool that knows itself."* Cities include **Zuevaron** (capital), **Arethon** (war front vs. Sahrimor), **Vulcaron**, **Marsatum/Victorath** (cold-war front vs. Vaeloris, near **The Stump**), **Apollyon** (knowledge city/**Guild Historia**). Military: Legions of **Ferratum/Ingenum/Impetum/Agilum**; contracted Kabal mages the **Magistratus**. Fighting a 15-year stalemated **Desert War** with Sahrimor and a generations-long cold war with Vaeloris.

- **Vaeloris & the Swamp Hegemony** — elven nation centered on **The Arbor** (largest tree in existence), ruled by **Sylvorn Vaelithar** plus **the Witnessed** (12 oracle-mages, secretly Kabal/Tertius-trained). Functions as a triarchy of elves, lizardfolk, and dragonborn (welcomed via **the Great Migration**, unknowingly the displaced co-founders of Thraekor's lost empire). Ten cities incl. **The Red Hold** (undefeated fortress), **The Confluence** (floating lizardfolk city), **The Swamp's Heart** (hides a lizardfolk hatchery). Military: **Rooted Legion** (Thornwatch archers, Rootwall, Devourers, Lurkers, Gargantua Riders, Druidic Spear) plus the swamp's separate **Heart Guard**.

- **Thraekor — "The Ash Confederation"** — dwarven clans in the Volcanic Mountain Range, rebuilt from **Dar-Rakvantim** (a dwarf-**dragonborn** coalition empire, secretly), destroyed when the **Pyreith-Kar'Mhal cult** magically triggered the volcano. Ruled by the **Stillwarden** (currently the failing **Khar-Sage Aelthrak**) from **Khar-Athel**, always a **Clan Aethrak** member via a rigged **Ritual Vote**. Core value: **the Ash Principle** ("what remains after everything burns"). Cities: Khar-Athel, Khar-Veth, Dunnrak, Khar-Dunn (contested bridge city), plus 7 city-states. Military doctrine: deliberately, proudly unfair (ambush, terrain destruction, night assault).

- **Norrvael — "The Isle of Silence and Drakes"** — island hidden by a permanent, origin-unknown **Mist**; absolute monarchy of **House Dravenkov** (300 years, 6 generations), ruling since **the Night of the Silent Choir** (a Fragmenta Majoris, the **Shard of Echoes**, was used to replace the old republic's court with doppelgangers before the god Nystros destroyed the impersonations). Current ruler **Empress Violetta Dravenkov** (bonded to drake **Lightning's Rend**, no heir). Enforcers: the masked **Silver Cloaks**. Elite drake-knights: **the Storm Riders**. Secretly, an ancient dragon sleeps beneath the island and is slowly waking — it is Drakenholm's/House Dravenkov's actual namesake and first ruler.

- **Sahrimor — "The Desert Commercial Empire"** — built around the inexplicable lake **Eternatum**, founded accidentally by merchant **Safir Halakum** ~572 years ago. Ruled by the **Merchant King** (currently **Salutin Seraphin**) via three monopolies (Water Law, Trade Legitimacy, and the **Unbound** sanctioned-violence mechanic), with eight color-coded **Merchant Lords**. Home to **the Mugamiir Safor** adventuring guild and the monster-haunted, mystery-shrouded **Black Sands** (a lost pre-war civilization's ruins). Fighting the same 15-year Desert War against Sanguivorum.

## 3. KEY NAMED CHARACTERS

**The Five Fingers:** Primus (secretly the last survivor of the Golden Plains, channels the Thirteenth via a bone fragment, engineered the Pyre of Peace); Secundus (spear-wielding battle master); Tertius/Dûrinne Stonebrow (exiled dwarf, unconsciously projects a different idealized appearance to everyone, can't see her own true face); Quartus (beloved public healer, secretly runs the horrific Garden of Remade Souls, obsessively fascinated with Tertius's hidden true form); Quintus (dragonborn teleportation master, taught secretly by the God of Time, has glimpsed apocalyptic futures and suspects Primus is central to them).

**Core adventuring party:** **Hydraxes Embercrest** (fugitive ex-guard, killed his corrupted friend Draconid, sister **Violeta** taken by the Kabal, seeking the lost city of Valen); **Zypher** (veteran elven Thornwatch archer, one-eyed); **Vorual** (572-year-old elf-human hybrid sealed in stone for 6 centuries, non-magical nature expert, huge, wields a drake-tooth axe); **Andreva** (Kabal necromancer/healer under Quartus, ambitious for his Finger seat, controls bone-constructs Anvil and Hammer); **Arcenn "Vaul"** (fallen-highborn thief hiding a stolen Kabal document that got his crew wiped out).

**Others:** **Violeta** (first mage ever attuned to the First River itself — unclassifiable); **Calrie Thorn** (Norrvael's doppelganger-bard catastrophe); **Varlen**, **Ithazuun**, **Captain Dereth** (hosts of three of the Six Great Fragmenta Majoris Events); **Imperator Alastair**, **Empress Violetta Dravenkov**, **Salutin Seraphin**, **Khar-Sage Aelthrak** (the four other national rulers); **Banta De-Lance** (fugitive-mage protector in Sahrimor); **Sorvaine Var-Kariden** (Thraekor clan leader plotting against Norrvael); twins **Aldric/Britta Stenn** (Norrvael bridge commanders); **Generalatus Hephaestus Horvatum** and **Aciduax** (rival border commanders on the Sanguivorum-Vaeloris cold-war front); **the Great Lizard** (mysterious swamp druid-lizardfolk).

## 4. MAGIC SYSTEM

All magic flows from **thirteen rivers** beneath the world: the **First River** (the Thirteenth's spilled blood — sourceless, moral-less, origin of Fragmenta) plus **twelve Divine Rivers** (one per Pantheon god). Mages can't touch a river directly — they use a **Conduit** (a personal anchor) which *defines* their classification into three **Paths**: **Study/Incisori** (built anchor, slow/stable growth, Kabal's preferred), **Faith/Offerendi** (belief-based multiplier, volatile, can spike under duress), **Nature/Germini** (found natural anchor, tied to environmental attunement, caster's personality drifts toward their river over time). Each mage also has a fixed **personal capacity ceiling** and an innate, self-discovered **River Attunement** (one of 13) plus malleable environmental attunement. Casting has three failure states: **Standard Exhaustion** (recoverable), **Conduit Severance** (catastrophic if mid-cast), and **Deep Immersion Failure** (overreach causes the river to "complete" the spell using the caster as material, symmetrically to the spell's intent). Ancient dragons and creatures like Sahrimor's **Ankaru** aren't mages at all but living saturations of original divine essence; Norrvael's **drakes** are diminished dragon-kin whose rising sentience stems from a nearby waking ancient dragon.

## 5. HISTORY / TIMELINE

Dar-Rakvantim (dwarf-dragonborn empire) thrives → **Great Mage War** (ends **572 years ago**) sees the Pyreith-Kar'Mhal cult destroy it and devastate the continent → **the Pyre of Peace** officially ends the war heroically but is secretly Primus's mass-sacrifice ritual that destroys his own homeland (the Golden Plains/Tormal) to seize the Thirteenth's skull → the **Gods' Hand Kabal** is founded on the ruins → Sanguivorum founded and rises over 570 years → Sahrimor founded by accident around Eternatum lake → Vaeloris's Kabal-directed "artifact hunt" triggers the 80-year Weeping Wood War → **~300 years ago** Norrvael's Night of the Silent Choir installs House Dravenkov → **~200 years ago** War of Red Wheat (Vaeloris vs. Sanguivorum) → **~130 years ago** Mugamiir Safor founded → **~112 years ago** Sahrimor's 32-year civil war ends (Seraphin victory) → **~53 years ago** Sanguivorum's Reclamation War/Enduring Storm ends (The Stump) → **15 years ago** an unresolved assassination attempt starts the still-ongoing Sanguivorum-Sahrimor Desert War → **present day**, with an ancient dragon stirring under Norrvael, a Thraekor succession crisis, and the new adventuring party fleeing/investigating the Kabal.

## 6. GAME-RELEVANT MECHANICS

Standardized cross-nation currency (gold/silver/iron/bronze/copper); mage registration + 10% **Mage Tithe**; the **Bruise** outlaw-mage/bounty system; the **Path/Conduit/River-Attunement** three-axis magic-build system; **Fragmenta tiers** as a ready-made artifact-rarity-with-risk system (plus a fusion mechanic); the Kabal's full rank/school/operational-arm progression ladder (Arcani Infant → Novitiate → Aspirant → Junior Adept → Adept → Senior Adept → Magi → Archmagi, across 5 schools and their operational arms); per-nation military rank ladders and unit rosters; a fully quantified **overland/sea travel system** (speeds by traveler type, terrain modifiers, seasonal modifiers, city-to-city route tables) — directly usable for a game's travel/logistics layer; two constructed languages (**Vaur'eth**, **Keth-Rakar**) with grammar/vocab for procedural names or inscriptions; 24 named legendary artifacts (a Zenith/Nadir pair per god); and six pre-built "legendary dungeon" hooks (the Six Great Majoris Events).

## 7. TONE / WRITING STYLE NOTES

Mythic, aphoristic, repetition-driven prose for cosmology/gods; dry analytical "worldbook" prose for institutions that consistently ends in a sharp, quotable cynical one-liner; constant deliberate dramatic irony via "Hidden History — What [Nation] Does Not Know" sections (GM-facing knowledge the in-world characters never learn); tight, present-tense "at a glance" character bios; a recurring device of stating a culture's apparent flaw and then reframing it as entirely internally coherent; heavy, specific numeric detail (exact ages/distances/populations/fees) used for texture as much as simulation; and deliberately incomplete "Still to Develop" threads left open throughout, signaling the material is meant to be built on rather than treated as closed canon.
